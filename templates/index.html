About Dataset & Methodology

UCI Machine Learning Repository Heart Disease (Cleveland Database) Documentation

Source & Overview

The dataset originates from the Cleveland Clinic Foundation, contributed by Dr. Robert Detrano, M.D., Ph.D. It is maintained within the UCI Machine Learning Repository (Heart Disease Database). While the comprehensive database contains 76 raw attributes, published research and benchmarks utilize this canonical 14-variable subset of 303 patient cases.

Reference Source: Detrano, R. et al. (1989). International application of a new probability algorithm for the diagnosis of coronary artery disease. American Journal of Cardiology, 64(5), 304-310.

Data Cleaning & Decision Protocol

  • Missing Value Handling: The dataset contained 4 missing values in ca and 2 in thal (encoded as ?). Rather than discarding 6 valid patient records, full records are preserved for univariate and demographic analyses.
  • Target Binarization: Original values 0 (absence) and 1, 2, 3, 4 (presence of coronary artery disease by angiographic severity) are standardly mapped: 0 → No Disease, ≥ 1 → Heart Disease.
  • Categorical Humanization: Encoded integers for chest pain type, biological sex, resting ECG, and thal are mapped to standardized clinical terms.

Canonical 14-Variable Data Dictionary

Attribute Clinical Definition Data Type Reference / Values
age Patient chronological age in years Numeric (Continuous) Range: 29 – 77 years
sex Biological sex of patient Categorical (Binary) 0 = Female, 1 = Male
cp Chest pain presentation category Categorical (Nominal) 1: Typical Angina, 2: Atypical Angina, 3: Non-Anginal, 4: Asymptomatic
trestbps Resting blood pressure on hospital admission Numeric (Continuous) Recorded in mm Hg (Normal: < 120 mm Hg)
chol Serum cholesterol concentration Numeric (Continuous) Recorded in mg/dl (Desirable: < 200 mg/dl)
fbs Fasting blood sugar > 120 mg/dl Categorical (Binary) 1 = True (> 120 mg/dl), 0 = False (≤ 120 mg/dl)
restecg Resting electrocardiographic results Categorical (Nominal) 0: Normal, 1: ST-T Abnormality, 2: LV Hypertrophy
thalach Maximum heart rate achieved during exercise treadmill test Numeric (Continuous) Recorded in beats per minute (bpm)
exang Exercise-induced angina symptom presence Categorical (Binary) 1 = Yes, 0 = No
oldpeak ST depression induced by exercise relative to rest Numeric (Continuous) Recorded in millimeters (mm) on ECG trace
slope Slope of peak exercise ST segment Categorical (Nominal) 1: Upsloping, 2: Flat, 3: Downsloping
ca Number of major cardiac vessels colored by fluoroscopy Numeric (Discrete) Range: 0 – 3 vessels
thal Thallium scintigraphy nuclear stress test defect status Categorical (Nominal) 3: Normal, 6: Fixed Defect, 7: Reversible Defect
target Diagnosis of coronary heart disease (> 50% angiographic diameter narrowing) Categorical (Binary Target) 0 = Absence (< 50% narrowing), 1 = Presence (> 50% narrowing)
